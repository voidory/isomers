Help

Ligands are idealised as points and chelate rings as symmetric bridges. Every isomer count is worked out from the symmetry of the shape, not typed in by hand. R/S labels for tetrahedral [MABCD] assume the made-up priority order A > B > C > D.

It always lands on an exam drawing

Let go and it settles on the nearest textbook view: wedges come toward you, hashes go away. The ↻ buttons turn it about its own symmetry axes.
